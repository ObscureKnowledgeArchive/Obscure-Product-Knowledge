# Automotive Repair Knowledge Archive

This section of the **Obscure Product Knowledge Archive** contains firsthand automotive repair records, diagnostic findings, troubleshooting information, and physically verified solutions.

The emphasis is on documenting actual problems encountered with specific vehicles and components, including symptoms, diagnostic observations, repairs performed, and the resulting behavior.

## Jeep

### Jeep Cherokee XJ

#### AW4 Automatic Transmission — Neutral Safety Switch

A documented intermittent no-start condition in a Jeep Cherokee XJ equipped with the AW4 automatic transmission. Rapidly moving the shifter temporarily restored starting, leading to diagnosis and repair of the neutral safety switch.

[Jeep Cherokee XJ AW4 No-Start — Neutral Safety Switch Sticky Grease Repair](/automotive/jeep/xj/aw4-neutral-safety-switch-sticky-grease-no-start/)

## About These Repair Records

Entries in this archive are based primarily on firsthand repair work and physical testing. A documented repair should not be interpreted to mean that every vehicle exhibiting the same symptom has the same underlying fault.

The records are intended to preserve difficult-to-find practical information for owners, technicians, restorers, and others troubleshooting automotive mechanical and electrical problems.
