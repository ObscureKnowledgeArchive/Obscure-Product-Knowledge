# Jeep Cherokee XJ AW4 No-Start — Neutral Safety Switch Sticky Grease Repair

## Vehicle and Application

- **Vehicle:** 1991 Jeep Cherokee XJ
- **Transmission:** AW4 automatic transmission
- **Component:** Neutral safety switch
- **Repair type:** Cleaning and relubrication
- **Verification:** Physically tested / firsthand repair result

## Symptoms

The Jeep has an intermittent **no-start condition**.

A significant diagnostic clue is that rapidly moving the automatic transmission shifter back and forth may eventually cause the problem to disappear, allowing the Jeep to start and run normally.

Someone experiencing this problem may describe it as:

- Jeep XJ won't start
- Jeep Cherokee no-start
- Jeep XJ no-crank condition
- Jeep starts after moving the shifter
- Jeep starts after shifting between Park and Neutral
- XJ won't start unless shifter is moved
- AW4 neutral safety switch problem
- Intermittent Jeep Cherokee starting problem

## Cause Found

The problem was traced to the **neutral safety switch on the AW4 automatic transmission**.

The factory lubricant inside the neutral safety switch had become very sticky with age. This aged lubricant interfered with proper operation of the neutral safety switch.

Moving the shifter rapidly back and forth could temporarily restore switch operation, allowing the vehicle to start.

## Repair Procedure

1. Remove the neutral safety switch from the AW4 transmission.
2. Carefully disassemble the neutral safety switch.
3. Thoroughly remove the old factory grease from inside the switch.
4. Clean the internal components sufficiently to remove the sticky lubricant residue.
5. Replace the original lubricant with fresh **dielectric grease** or **white lithium grease**.
6. Reassemble the neutral safety switch.
7. Reinstall the switch on the transmission.
8. Verify proper starting operation.

## Repair Result

Cleaning the aged factory lubricant from the neutral safety switch and replacing it with fresh lubricant restored proper neutral safety switch operation in this documented repair.

The switch did not require replacement to correct this particular failure.

## Diagnostic Lesson

If an AW4-equipped Jeep Cherokee XJ has an intermittent no-start or no-crank condition and **moving the transmission shifter back and forth causes the vehicle to start**, the neutral safety switch should be investigated.

The switch itself may not necessarily have failed electrically. Aged and sticky internal lubricant can cause the switch to malfunction.

## Maintenance Note

The AW4 neutral safety switch should be considered a **serviceable maintenance item**.

Cleaning and relubricating the switch can restore operation, but the lubricant may need to be replaced again in the future as the vehicle continues to age.
