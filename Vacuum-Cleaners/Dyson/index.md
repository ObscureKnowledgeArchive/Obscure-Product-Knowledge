# Vacuum Cleaner Repair Knowledge Archive

This section of the **Obscure Product Knowledge Archive** contains firsthand repair records, troubleshooting information, modifications, and physically tested solutions for consumer vacuum cleaners.

The emphasis is on documenting actual problems encountered with specific models, including symptoms, diagnostic observations, repairs or modifications performed, and the resulting behavior.

## Dyson

### Dyson Ball Animal 2 / UP20

A documented solution for excessive cleaner-head suction that can make the vacuum extremely difficult to push on certain carpets. Testing included multiple spacer thicknesses to determine their effect on suction and usability.

## About These Repair Records

Entries in this archive are based primarily on firsthand repair work and physical testing. A documented repair or modification should not be interpreted to mean that every machine exhibiting the same symptom has the same underlying cause.

The records are intended to preserve difficult-to-find practical information for owners, technicians, repairers, and others troubleshooting consumer vacuum cleaners.
