# Obscure Product Knowledge Archive

A public reference library for obscure, difficult-to-find information about consumer products, parts, repairs, compatibility, specifications, and other technical details.

## Why This Archive Exists

Some product information is surprisingly difficult to find.

Service manuals disappear. Manufacturers discontinue parts. Part numbers are incorrectly cross-referenced. Old forum posts contradict one another. Replacement-parts sellers sometimes list incorrect compatibility information. Valuable technical knowledge can become buried in discussions or disappear entirely.

This archive exists to preserve useful answers once they have been researched, documented, measured, or physically verified.

The focus is not information that can already be found easily. The focus is the obscure fact that took hours of research, original documentation, physical inspection, experimentation, or repair work to establish.

## Evidence Standards

Entries should clearly distinguish between what is known and what is still uncertain.

### VERIFIED
Supported by strong documentation and physically confirmed through inspection, measurement, installation, testing, or operation.

### DOCUMENTED
Supported by authoritative documentation such as manufacturer service manuals, parts catalogs, technical bulletins, or other primary sources, but not physically verified by the contributor.

### TESTED
Physically tested or measured successfully, but authoritative manufacturer documentation has not been located.

### PROBABLE
Multiple pieces of evidence strongly support the information, but definitive documentation or physical verification is still lacking.

### UNVERIFIED
Potentially useful information or a lead that has not yet been sufficiently confirmed.

## What Belongs Here

Examples include:

- Obsolete or difficult-to-find replacement part numbers
- Belt, gear, bearing, capacitor, and component specifications
- Consumer electronics repair information
- Vintage audio equipment
- Appliance parts and repairs
- Automotive part compatibility
- Arcade and gaming hardware
- Replacement components for discontinued products
- Manufacturer service information
- Known incorrect cross-references
- Physical measurements of unavailable parts
- Tested substitutions and workarounds
- Model-specific differences
- Other obscure consumer-product information that is unusually difficult to locate

## Philosophy

Accuracy is more important than having an answer.

Whenever possible, entries identify the source of the information and distinguish manufacturer documentation from physical testing, secondary sources, and inference.

Incorrect information should be corrected when better evidence becomes available, while GitHub's revision history preserves the history of those changes.

## Contributing

Corrections and additional evidence are welcome.

When proposing a correction, please provide supporting evidence whenever possible, such as manufacturer documentation, photographs, measurements, part markings, or the results of physical testing.
