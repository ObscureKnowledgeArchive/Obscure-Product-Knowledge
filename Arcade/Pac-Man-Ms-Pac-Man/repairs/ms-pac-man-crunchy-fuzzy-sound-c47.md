# Crunchy and Fuzzy Sound Effects


## Repair Case Summary

This page documents a firsthand **Crunchy and Fuzzy Sound Effects** repair case.
It is intended for technicians and collectors troubleshooting original Pac-Man or Ms. Pac-Man arcade PCB hardware.

- **Date:** 2018-06-05
- **Board:** Original Ms. Pac-Man PCB
- **Category:** Sound / capacitor
- **Symptoms:**
  - Every sound effect was recognizable but sounded crunchy and fuzzy.
- **Diagnostic observations / tests:**
  - Resistors and capacitors in the sound circuit were checked.
  - Capacitor C47 measured far out of range.
- **Components / locations investigated:** C47, sound circuit capacitors, sound circuit resistors
- **Root cause:** C47 was out of range.
- **Repair:** Replaced C47 with a used capacitor from a parts board.
- **Result:** Sound effects returned to normal.
- **Search terms:** Ms Pac-Man crunchy sound; fuzzy sound; distorted sound; C47; capacitor; sound circuit
- **Evidence:** Physically verified repair / firsthand repair log
