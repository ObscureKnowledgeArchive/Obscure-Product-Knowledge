# Screen Full of Red 2s Caused by Loose Sync Bus Controller


## Repair Case Summary

This page documents a firsthand **Screen Full of Red 2s Caused by Loose Sync Bus Controller** repair case.
It is intended for technicians and collectors troubleshooting original Pac-Man or Ms. Pac-Man arcade PCB hardware.

- **Date:** 2017-04-08
- **Board:** Original Pac-Man PCB
- **Category:** Boot failure / daughter card connection
- **Symptoms:**
  - Entire screen filled with red 2s at startup.
  - Pressing on the Z80 made no change.
  - Pressing down on the Z80 Sync Bus Controller caused the board to boot normally.
- **Diagnostic observations / tests:**
  - The Sync Bus Controller card had been bumped and was not strapped down.
- **Components / locations investigated:** Z80 Sync Bus Controller card
- **Root cause:** Poor connection at the Sync Bus Controller card.
- **Repair:** Pressed the Sync Bus Controller firmly into place and secured it with a zip tie.
- **Result:** No further problems were observed.
- **Search terms:** Pac-Man red 2s; screen full of 2s; Sync Bus Controller; Z80 daughter card; loose card; boot failure
- **Evidence:** Physically verified repair / firsthand repair log
