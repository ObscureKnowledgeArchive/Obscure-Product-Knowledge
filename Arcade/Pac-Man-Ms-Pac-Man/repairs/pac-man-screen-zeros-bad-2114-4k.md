# Screen Full of Zeros With Irregular Black Blocks


## Repair Case Summary

This page documents a firsthand **Screen Full of Zeros With Irregular Black Blocks** repair case.
It is intended for technicians and collectors troubleshooting original Pac-Man or Ms. Pac-Man arcade PCB hardware.

- **Date:** 2017-04-07
- **Board:** Original Pac-Man PCB
- **Category:** Scratchpad RAM / boot failure
- **Symptoms:**
  - Startup screen full of 0s.
  - Irregular blocks of black space appeared among the zeros.
- **Diagnostic observations / tests:**
  - All scratchpad RAM from 4R through 4K was temporarily replaced with a known-good set and the board booted.
  - The removed 2114 RAMs were tested individually.
  - The failure could be reproduced with the bad RAM installed at 4K.
- **Components / locations investigated:** 4R-4K scratchpad RAM, 4K, 2114 RAM
- **Root cause:** One bad 2114 scratchpad RAM, originally at 4K.
- **Repair:** Replaced the bad 2114 RAM.
- **Result:** The board booted normally.
- **Search terms:** Pac-Man screen full of zeros; 0s on screen; black blocks; 2114 RAM; scratchpad RAM; 4K; 4R; boot failure
- **Evidence:** Physically verified repair / firsthand repair log
