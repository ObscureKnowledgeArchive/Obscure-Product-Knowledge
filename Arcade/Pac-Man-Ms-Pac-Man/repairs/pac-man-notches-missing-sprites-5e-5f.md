# Notches Missing From Pac-Man and Ghost Graphics


## Repair Case Summary

This page documents a firsthand **Notches Missing From Pac-Man and Ghost Graphics** repair case.
It is intended for technicians and collectors troubleshooting original Pac-Man or Ms. Pac-Man arcade PCB hardware.

- **Date:** 2017-04-07
- **Board:** Original Pac-Man PCB
- **Category:** Graphics / character ROMs
- **Symptoms:**
  - Pac-Man had a notch missing on the side opposite his mouth.
  - All ghosts had a notch missing from the tops of their heads.
- **Diagnostic observations / tests:**
  - Original masked ROMs were in use.
  - Swapping the suspect ROMs removed the problem.
- **Components / locations investigated:** 5E, 5F
- **Root cause:** Bad 5E/5F masked ROMs.
- **Repair:** Replaced the original masked ROMs at 5E/5F with known-good ROMs.
- **Result:** Pac-Man and ghost graphics returned to normal.
- **Search terms:** Pac-Man notch; ghost head notch; missing sprite pixels; 5E; 5F; masked ROM; character graphics
- **Evidence:** Physically verified repair / firsthand repair log
