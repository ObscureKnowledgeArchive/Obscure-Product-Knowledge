# Sparkles or Stars Around Pac-Man and Ghosts


## Repair Case Summary

This page documents a firsthand **Sparkles or Stars Around Pac-Man and Ghosts** repair case.
It is intended for technicians and collectors troubleshooting original Pac-Man or Ms. Pac-Man arcade PCB hardware.

- **Date:** 2017-04-07
- **Board:** Original Pac-Man PCB
- **Category:** Graphics / character ROMs
- **Symptoms:**
  - Sparkles/stars visible during attract mode when Pac-Man and the ghosts appear.
  - Sparkles/stars visible in the ghost pen/camp on the maze screen.
- **Diagnostic observations / tests:**
  - Suspect 5E/5F devices were tried on another board and produced the same symptoms.
- **Components / locations investigated:** 5E, 5F
- **Root cause:** Bad devices/ROMs at 5E/5F.
- **Repair:** Replaced the bad 5E/5F devices with known-good parts.
- **Result:** The graphical sparkles/stars were eliminated.

- **Evidence:** Physically verified repair / firsthand repair log
