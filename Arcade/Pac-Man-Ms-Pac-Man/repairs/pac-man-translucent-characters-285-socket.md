# Intermittent Translucent Characters and Jumping Ghosts From Bad 285 Socket


## Repair Case Summary

This page documents a firsthand **Intermittent Translucent Characters and Jumping Ghosts From Bad 285 Socket** repair case.
It is intended for technicians and collectors troubleshooting original Pac-Man or Ms. Pac-Man arcade PCB hardware.

- **Date:** 2018-06-07
- **Board:** Original Pac-Man PCB
- **Category:** Socket / intermittent graphics
- **Symptoms:**
  - Two Pac-Man characters briefly appeared toward the upper-left.
  - Multiple ghosts jumped around the screen.
  - During the glitch, characters appeared almost translucent.
- **Diagnostic observations / tests:**
  - The 285 had previously been inserted without the sweep, enlarging the individual socket receptacles.
  - The 285 barely made contact in the damaged socket.
- **Components / locations investigated:** 285 socket, 285
- **Root cause:** Damaged/loose 285 socket.
- **Repair:** Replaced the bad 285 socket.
- **Result:** The socket-related graphical instability was corrected.
- **Search terms:** Pac-Man 285 socket; translucent characters; ghosts jumping; two Pac-Man characters; intermittent graphics; bad socket
- **Evidence:** Physically verified repair / firsthand repair log
