# Missing Background Siren and Hum With Aftermarket Ms. Pac-Man Daughter Card


## Repair Case Summary

This page documents a firsthand **Missing Background Siren and Hum With Aftermarket Ms. Pac-Man Daughter Card** repair case.
It is intended for technicians and collectors troubleshooting original Pac-Man or Ms. Pac-Man arcade PCB hardware.

- **Date:** 2018-06-09
- **Board:** Original Pac-Man PCB with aftermarket Ms. Pac-Man daughter card
- **Category:** Sound / ROMs / daughter card
- **Symptoms:**
  - Ms. Pac-Man booted and played but background sounds were missing.
  - C49 was visibly missing.
  - With the daughter card removed and the board run as Pac-Man, background sirens were present but a humming noise remained while the custom 6F ROM was installed.
- **Diagnostic observations / tests:**
  - Replacing C49 alone made no difference.
  - The aftermarket daughter card used custom-burned ROMs, and 6F also contained a custom ROM.
  - The board was cleaned and custom ROMs were replaced with factory units; the hum disappeared.
- **Components / locations investigated:** C49, 6F, aftermarket Ms. Pac-Man daughter card, custom ROMs
- **Root cause:** Problem associated with custom-burned ROMs, with C49 also missing.
- **Repair:** Removed the custom-burned ROMs, restored factory ROMs, and replaced C49.
- **Result:** All sound issues were resolved.
- **Search terms:** Ms Pac-Man missing siren; background sound missing; hum; aftermarket daughter card; custom ROM; 6F; C49
- **Evidence:** Physically verified repair / firsthand repair log
