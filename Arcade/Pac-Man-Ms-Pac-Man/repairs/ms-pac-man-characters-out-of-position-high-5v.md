# Ms. Pac-Man and Ghosts Out of Position From High +5 V Supply


## Repair Case Summary

This page documents a firsthand **Ms. Pac-Man and Ghosts Out of Position From High +5 V Supply** repair case.
It is intended for technicians and collectors troubleshooting original Pac-Man or Ms. Pac-Man arcade PCB hardware.

- **Date:** 2017-06-17
- **Board:** Original Ms. Pac-Man PCB and daughter card
- **Category:** Power / sprite positioning
- **Symptoms:**
  - PCB and daughter card worked correctly on the test rig for an hour.
  - When installed in a Pac-Man cabinet using a switching power supply, ghosts hovered at the top of the screen.
  - Ms. Pac-Man jumped around the screen during play.
- **Diagnostic observations / tests:**
  - Switcher output measured nearly 5.40 V.
  - Reducing the supply to 5.00 V restored normal operation.
- **Components / locations investigated:** Switching power supply, +5 V rail
- **Root cause:** Switcher power supply adjusted too high at approximately 5.40 V.
- **Repair:** Reduced switcher output from approximately 5.40 V to 5.00 V.
- **Result:** The game worked normally.

- **Evidence:** Physically verified repair / firsthand repair log
