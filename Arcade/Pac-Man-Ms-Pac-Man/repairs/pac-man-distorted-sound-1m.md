# Slightly Distorted Sound Effects and Intermission Music


## Repair Case Summary

This page documents a firsthand **Slightly Distorted Sound Effects and Intermission Music** repair case.
It is intended for technicians and collectors troubleshooting original Pac-Man or Ms. Pac-Man arcade PCB hardware.

- **Date:** 2017-06-17
- **Board:** Original Pac-Man PCB
- **Category:** Sound
- **Symptoms:**
  - Background siren was slightly distorted.
  - Intermission music was slightly distorted.
  - All test-mode sounds were audible but distorted.
- **Diagnostic observations / tests:**
  - Replacing the volume switch produced no change.
  - Swapping 1N produced no change.
  - Working backward through the sound circuit led from 1N to 2M to 1M.
  - 1M was factory socketed, so it was swapped.
- **Components / locations investigated:** Volume switch, 1N, 2M, 1M
- **Root cause:** Fault at 1M.
- **Repair:** Swapped the device at 1M.
- **Result:** All sound effects became distortion-free and clear.
- **Search terms:** Pac-Man distorted sound; distorted siren; intermission distortion; 1M; 1N; 2M; sound circuit
- **Evidence:** Physically verified repair / firsthand repair log
