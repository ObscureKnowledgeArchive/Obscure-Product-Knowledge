# Pac-Man and Ghosts Stuck in Upper-Left Corner


## Repair Case Summary

This page documents a firsthand **Pac-Man and Ghosts Stuck in Upper-Left Corner** repair case.
It is intended for technicians and collectors troubleshooting original Pac-Man or Ms. Pac-Man arcade PCB hardware.

- **Date:** 2017-06-07
- **Board:** Original Pac-Man PCB
- **Category:** Sprite positioning
- **Symptoms:**
  - Pac-Man and all ghosts appeared in the upper-left corner.
  - The problem occurred during attract mode and during play.
- **Diagnostic observations / tests:**
  - The 7489 devices at 2F and 2H were checked first.
  - Piggybacking 2H with a known-good chip made no difference.
  - Piggybacking 2F caused the screen to attempt to correct itself.
- **Components / locations investigated:** 2F, 2H, 7489
- **Root cause:** Bad 7489 at 2F.
- **Repair:** Removed the old 7489 at 2F, installed a socket, and installed a replacement chip.
- **Result:** The board worked perfectly.
- **Search terms:** Pac-Man upper left corner; ghosts upper left; sprite position; 2F; 2H; 7489; characters stuck corner
- **Evidence:** Physically verified repair / firsthand repair log
