# Zurn / Wilkins BR4 Pressure Reducing Valve — Leak Repair and RK1-BR4 Rebuild

**Product:** Zurn / Wilkins BR4 Pressure Reducing Valve — circa 2003  
**Size:** 1 inch  
**Repair kit:** RK1-BR4 — discontinued  
**Symptom:** Water leaking from the upper bell / top bolt area  
**Repair status:** VERIFIED — REBUILT AND WORKING  
**Final regulated pressure:** 50 PSI  
**Evidence:** Firsthand repair, physical inspection, installation, and operational testing

---

## Quick Answer

A leaking 1-inch Zurn / Wilkins BR4 pressure reducing valve was successfully repaired using the **RK1-BR4 repair kit**.

The valve was leaking from the upper portion of the regulator near the black bell and top bolt. The leak became noticeably worse when the sprinkler system was operating.

After disassembly, the original internal sealing components showed age-related deterioration. The old gaskets/seals were dry and produced black residue during disassembly.

The BR4 was rebuilt using the RK1-BR4 repair kit rather than replacing the complete pressure reducing valve.

After reassembly and adjustment:

- The leak was eliminated.
- The regulator maintained approximately **50 PSI**.
- No additional leakage was observed.
- The repair was considered successful.

**Result: RK1-BR4 rebuild — PHYSICALLY INSTALLED — VERIFIED WORKING**

---

## Original Symptom

The installed BR4 pressure reducing valve developed a water leak from the upper portion of the regulator.

The leak appeared to originate around the **top bolt / black bell area**.

An additional observation was that the leak became substantially worse while the home's sprinkler system was operating.

This is documented as an observation only. Increased water demand coincided with increased leakage, but this repair does not establish that sprinkler operation caused the failure.

## Repair Kit Availability

**Important:** The Zurn / Wilkins **RK1-BR4 rebuild kit has been discontinued**.

At the time of this documented repair, new-old-stock and remaining inventory of the RK1-BR4 kit could still be found from some suppliers. However, because the kit is no longer in regular production, availability may become increasingly limited and the kit may be difficult to obtain.

Owners attempting to rebuild a BR4 should verify that a listed kit is specifically the **RK1-BR4** and should not assume that another Zurn / Wilkins repair kit is interchangeable unless compatibility has been independently confirmed.

The successful repair documented on this page used an actual **RK1-BR4 repair kit**.

## Findings During Disassembly

Physical inspection during the rebuild found deterioration consistent with aged internal sealing components.

Notable observations included:

- Existing gaskets/seals were dry.
- Black residue was present from the deteriorated sealing material.
- The regulator could be disassembled and serviced rather than requiring complete valve replacement.

A useful discovery during the repair was that removal of the upper bell provides greater access to the replaceable internal components than may initially be apparent when examining the installed valve.

---

## Repair Performed

The existing BR4 remained installed in the plumbing system and was rebuilt using the RK1-BR4 repair components.

The repair consisted of disassembling the serviceable portion of the pressure reducing valve, removing the aged components, installing the appropriate replacement components from the rebuild kit, reassembling the regulator, and readjusting the outlet pressure.

The complete valve body did not require replacement.

## What This Repair Confirms

This firsthand repair confirms that a leaking 1-inch Zurn / Wilkins BR4 pressure reducing valve can, in at least this failure case, be successfully rebuilt using the **RK1-BR4 repair kit** without replacing the complete regulator.

It also confirms that leakage appearing around the upper bell or top-bolt area does not necessarily mean that the entire valve body must be replaced.

The exact source of a leak should still be diagnosed before assuming that another BR4 has the same failure.

Because the RK1-BR4 repair kit has been discontinued, the practical ability to perform this repair will depend on locating remaining inventory or new-old-stock kits.

---

## Important Limitations

This entry documents one physically completed repair and should not be interpreted to mean that every leaking BR4 can be repaired with a rebuild kit.

A damaged, cracked, severely corroded, or otherwise compromised valve body may require complete replacement.

Water should be shut off and system pressure relieved before servicing a pressure reducing valve. Local plumbing requirements may also apply.
